About Ember Box Studio

Who we are

Ember Box Studio is an independent studio dedicated to creating 2D pixel art games with great care and attention to detail. We were born from a passion for classic games and a desire to tell simple, fun stories full of personality.

Our mascot, Mel the cat, is the star of all our games — from space platformers to adventures among ancient pyramids.

Our mission

We want to create accessible, nostalgic and charming pixel art experiences for players of all ages — on any platform.

Every game is crafted with care, from the pixel to the soundtrack.

Our values

Art made with love

Every sprite is hand-drawn, pixel by pixel.

Memorable characters

Mel joins the player in every adventure.

Fun comes first

Games that are easy to learn and fun to play.

Independent studio

Made by people who are passionate about games.
