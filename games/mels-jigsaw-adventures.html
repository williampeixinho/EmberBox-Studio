Mel's Jigsaw Adventures

Mel's Jigsaw Adventures is a cozy jigsaw puzzle game set in the world of Mel the Cat. Relax with hand-drawn pixel art scenes from space, forests, deserts and snowy mountains, with Mel by your side to cheer you on, give you hints and tell the story behind every picture. No timer, no pressure — just you, the pieces and a good controller.

Highlights

  • 40 hand-drawn pixel art illustrations across 4 themed worlds
  • Three difficulty levels, from relaxing 24-piece puzzles to bigger challenges
  • Mel helps with hints, reactions and stories about each scene
  • Hidden paw prints, a collectible gallery and plenty of achievements
  • Made for gamepad first, with full mouse and keyboard support
  • Available in 9 languages
