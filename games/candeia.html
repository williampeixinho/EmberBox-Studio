Candeia

Candeia is a puzzle-platformer built on one rule: what the light touches stops in time, and what stops becomes ground. Guide a hooded girl with an oil lamp through a dark cave, freezing falling rain into stepping stones, rocks into a staircase and even the shadows that hunt her into springboards. But the dark matters too: lifts, wheels and tides only move when the lamp is off.

Highlights

  • 50 levels across five regions of the cave, ending in a chase against the Great Shade
  • Candles, crystals, mirrors, gears, wind, will-o'-wisps and searchlights give the light new uses
  • 150 hidden fireflies, time medals and a secret level, the Firefly Garden
  • A cave map that lights up, lantern by lantern, as you go deeper
  • A short illustrated story that opens and closes the journey
  • Assist mode, hints at the right moment and full controller support
